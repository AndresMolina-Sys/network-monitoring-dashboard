## THESIS

A carefully edited research surface lets network operators and portfolio reviewers scan node health, then inspect the evidence behind each simulated measurement.

## OWN-WORLD

Applied Research Journal: warm charcoal, paper-toned type, copper accents, and sea-glass or amber only for meaningful status. Use restrained rules and compact annotations; keep fixture data explicitly simulated.

## STORY

From the inventory, identify a node and its status; open it, select 1h, 24h, or 7d, and compare the summary, charts, and table. This demonstrates a full-stack portfolio flow, not live monitoring.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At 1440x900, `/` uses a centered 1180px shell: slim “Network operations / Simulated inventory” bar, “Monitored nodes” and count, search/status controls, then three aligned roster rows ending in “Open metrics”. `/nodes/:nodeId` uses a compact breadcrumb and node identity, range selector with “Simulated data”, three KPIs, latency as the main chart, packet loss with an actual maximum-sample annotation in the side margin, and the measurements table below. Signature interaction: choosing a range updates the summary, both charts, and table together. Motion: 150ms ease-out for selection, focus, and state transitions only; remove nonessential motion for reduced-motion preferences.
